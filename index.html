WELCOME TO OUR SCHOOL

A school where every child is encouraged to learn, lead and grow.

Harsit Mission School is a Nursery to Class 10 school located in Sainik Colony, Dumardaga, Booty, Ranchi. Since 2003, the school has been building a learning environment where academic development, discipline, confidence and good values can grow together.

Our classrooms and school activities are designed to give children opportunities to learn with curiosity, participate with confidence and develop the habits needed for a responsible future.

Harsit Mission School Committed to every learner.
ACADEMICS

From first steps to Class 10.

Age-appropriate learning from the foundational years through secondary school, with a focus on strong basics, participation and all-round development. The academic approach follows CBSE pattern, with Class 10 preparation aligned to both CBSE and JAC examination patterns.

01

Early Years

Nursery, LKG & UKG

Playful, caring and activity-led foundations.
02

Primary School

Classes I – V

Strong fundamentals, curiosity and confident participation.
03

Middle School

Classes VI – VIII

Deeper understanding, discipline and independent learning.
04

Secondary School

Classes IX – X

CBSE-patterned learning with Class 10 preparation for both CBSE and JAC examination patterns.
